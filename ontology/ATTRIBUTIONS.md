## Attribution and third-party licenses

VitalGraph reuses the following resources.

- **Records in Contexts Ontology (RiC-O) 1.1**, developed by the International Council on Archives Expert Group on Archival Description (ICA EGAD). Copyright 2019–, International Council on Archives. Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). VitalGraph extracts selected classes and properties and omits selected annotations. [RiC-O source and documentation](https://www.ica.org/standards/RiC/RiC-O_1-1.html)

- **NCI Thesaurus (NCIt), release 26.10d**, provided by the National Cancer Institute. Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). VitalGraph reuses selected concept identifiers, labels, and definitions for event categories. [NCIt terms of use](https://evs.nci.nih.gov/ftp1/NCI_Thesaurus/ThesaurusTermsofUse.htm)

- **Person ontology**, part of the MARIO Ontology Network. Licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). VitalGraph extracts selected name and gender properties, related classes, and gender values. [Source repository and license](https://github.com/luigi-asprino/MON)

- **FOAF Vocabulary**, by Dan Brickley and Libby Miller. Copyright © 2000–2014 Dan Brickley and Libby Miller. The specification and accompanying RDF documentation are licensed under [CC BY 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/). VitalGraph extracts `foaf:age` and its domain definition. [FOAF specification](https://xmlns.com/foaf/spec/)

- **RDF and RDF Schema (RDFS)**, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). VitalGraph uses these standards to describe resources, types, labels, and class hierarchies. Its dictionary includes `rdf:type`, `rdfs:label`, `rdfs:subClassOf`, `rdfs:seeAlso`, `rdfs:Resource`, and `rdfs:Literal`. [RDF specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf11-concepts/) · [RDFS specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/rdf-schema/)

The glossary and Armenian-label dictionary include English labels and definitions from the resources credited above. VitalGraph-specific explanations and Armenian translations are project additions. Armenian labels are being prepared and reviewed, with review status tracked in the [glossary of terms](../VG-glossary-of-terms-hy.md).
